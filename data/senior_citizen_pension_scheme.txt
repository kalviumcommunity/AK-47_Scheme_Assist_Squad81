NATIONAL SOCIAL ASSISTANCE PROGRAMME (NSAP)
INDIRA GANDHI NATIONAL OLD AGE PENSION SCHEME (IGNOAPS) GUIDELINES

1. Background and Purpose:
The Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS) is a non-contributory pension scheme under the Ministry of Rural Development aimed at providing monthly financial assistance to senior citizens living Below Poverty Line (BPL).

2. Eligibility Conditions:
- The age of the applicant must be 60 years or higher.
- The applicant must belong to a household living Below Poverty Line (BPL) according to criteria prescribed by the Government of India.
- Permanent residence certificate and active bank account seeded with Aadhaar are mandatory.

3. Scale of Financial Assistance:
- For persons aged 60 to 79 years: Central assistance of Rs 200 per month per beneficiary (States supplement this with an additional state share, making the total pension between Rs 1,000 to Rs 2,500 per month).
- For persons aged 80 years and above: Central assistance of Rs 500 per month per beneficiary.

4. Application and Verification:
- Applications can be submitted online via the NSAP portal (https://nsap.nic.in) or offline through Gram Panchayat / Urban Local Body offices.
- Gram Sabha conducts social audit and verifies BPL list status.
- Sanction order issued by Sub-Divisional Officer (SDO) / Block Development Officer (BDO).

5. Grievance Redressal:
- District Social Welfare Officer acts as the primary grievance authority.
- Grievances must be resolved within 30 days of submission.
