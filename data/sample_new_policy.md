# Pradhan Mantri Vishwakarma Kaushal Samman Yojana (Official Guidelines)

## 1. Scheme Overview & Objectives
The Pradhan Mantri Vishwakarma Scheme is a holistic central sector welfare scheme launched by the Government of India to provide end-to-end support to traditional artisans and craftspeople who work with their hands and traditional tools. The scheme aims to strengthen and nurture the Guru-Shishya parampara and scale the products and services of Vishwakarma beneficiaries into domestic and international value chains.

## 2. Eligible Traditional Trades
The scheme encompasses 18 traditional trades across rural and urban India:
- Wood-based: Carpenter (Suthar), Boat Maker.
- Iron/Metal-based: Blacksmith (Lohar), Armourer, Locksmith, Sculptor (Moortikar/Stone Carver), Stone Breaker.
- Gold/Silver-based: Goldsmith (Sonar).
- Clay/Pottery-based: Potter (Kumhaar).
- Leather-based: Cobbler (Charmakar) / Shoesmith / Footwear artisan.
- Construction-based: Mason (Rajmistri).
- Other Crafts: Basket/Mat/Broom Maker / Coir Weaver, Doll & Toy Maker (Traditional), Barber (Naai), Garland maker (Malakaar), Washerman (Dhobi), Tailor (Darzi), and Fishing Net Maker.

## 3. Financial Assistance and Benefits
Beneficiaries registered under PM Vishwakarma receive a structured package of benefits:
- **Skill Training & Stipend**: 5-7 days of basic skill training with a daily stipend allowance of Rs 500 per day.
- **Modern Toolkit Incentive**: A direct financial incentive grant of Rs 15,000 provided in the form of an e-voucher or DBT to purchase modern trade toolkits.
- **Collateral-Free Credit Support**: Enterprise development loans up to Rs 3,00,000 provided in two tranches without any collateral requirement.
  - First tranche: Up to Rs 1,00,000 repayable over 18 months.
  - Second tranche: Up to Rs 2,00,000 for beneficiaries who maintain standard repayment accounts.
  - Concessional Interest Rate: Fixed interest rate of 5 percent with interest subvention cap of 8 percent paid directly by the Ministry.
- **Digital Transaction Incentive**: Cash incentive of Re 1 per digital transaction for up to 100 transactions per month.

## 4. Eligibility Criteria
To register for PM Vishwakarma benefits:
1. The applicant must be an artisan or craftsperson working on a self-employment basis in one of the 18 specified traditional family-based trades.
2. The minimum age of the beneficiary should be 18 years on the date of registration.
3. The benefit is restricted to one member per household (defined as husband, wife, and unmarried children).
4. Persons in government service and their family members are strictly ineligible.
5. The applicant must not have availed loans under similar credit-based welfare schemes (such as PMEGP, PM SVANidhi, Mudra) in the past 5 years.

## 5. Application and Verification Process
Enrolment is conducted via Common Service Centers (CSCs) on the PM Vishwakarma portal:
- Stage 1: Village Panchayat or Urban Local Body (ULB) verification.
- Stage 2: District Implementation Committee screening.
- Stage 3: Final approval by the National Steering Committee.
Upon successful verification, artisans receive a digital PM Vishwakarma Certificate and ID Card.
