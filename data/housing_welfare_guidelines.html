Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) Housing Guidelines

1. Mission Objective

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana addresses urban and rural housing shortages among Economically Weaker Sections (EWS), Low Income Groups (LIG), and rural homeless households by ensuring a pucca house with basic amenities to all eligible families.

2. Eligibility Criteria

  • The beneficiary family should not own a pucca house anywhere in India.
  • Economically Weaker Section (EWS) annual household income up to Rs 3,00,000.
  • Low Income Group (LIG) annual household income between Rs 3,00,001 and Rs 6,00,000.
  • Middle Income Group (MIG-I & MIG-II) income bands as notified by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.

3. Subsidy and Financial Assistance

Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS) provides upfront interest subsidy of up to 6.5% on home loans up to Rs 6,00,000 for a tenure of 20 years. Rural beneficiaries under PMAY-G receive direct construction assistance of Rs 1,20,000 in plain areas and Rs 1,30,000 in hilly/difficult states.

4. Mandatory House Features

  • Minimum carpet area of 25 square meters with hygienic cooking space.
  • Mandatory toilet construction under Swachh Bharat Mission convergence.
  • Electricity connection under Saubhagya and LPG connection under Ujjwala Scheme.

5. Implementation and Monitoring

Geo-tagging of construction stages via the AwaasApp mobile application. Direct Benefit Transfer into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts across four milestone stages.
