# Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY) Policy Document

## 1. Executive Summary
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the world's largest government funded healthcare assurance scheme, providing a health cover of Rs 5 Lakhs per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization to over 12 Crore poor and vulnerable families.

## 2. Coverage & Benefit Package
- Cashless and paperless access to healthcare services for the beneficiary at the point of service (in empanelled hospitals).
- Cover includes 3 days of pre-hospitalization and 15 days of post-hospitalization expenses such as diagnostics and medicines.
- No restriction on family size, age, or gender.
- All pre-existing conditions are covered from day one.
- Benefits of the scheme are portable across the country (a beneficiary can visit any empanelled public or private hospital in India).

## 3. Beneficiary Identification & Eligibility Criteria
- Identified based on the deprivation and occupational criteria of Socio-Economic and Caste Census 2011 (SECC 2011) for rural and urban areas respectively.
- Rural Criteria: Households living in one room with kucha walls/roof, female-headed households with no adult male member aged 16-59, disabled members, SC/ST households, landless manual casual labor.
- Urban Criteria: 11 defined occupational categories (ragpickers, domestic workers, street vendors, transport workers, construction workers, etc.).

## 4. Hospital Network & Empanelled Procedures
- Covers approximately 1,949 treatment procedures across 27 specialties including oncology, neurosurgery, cardiology, and orthopaedics.
- Over 29,000 public and private hospitals empanelled nationwide.
- Digital Golden Card issuance via Ayushman Mitra helpdesks at hospital premises.
