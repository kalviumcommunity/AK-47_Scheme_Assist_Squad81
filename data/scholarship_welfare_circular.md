# National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS) Circular

## 1. Scheme Objective
The objective of the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme is to award scholarships to meritorious students of economically weaker sections to arrest their drop out at class VIII and encourage them to continue study at secondary stage.

## 2. Quantum and Duration of Scholarship
- A scholarship amount of Rs 12,000/- per annum (Rs 1,000/- per month) is provided to selected students from Class IX to Class XII in State Government, Government-aided, and Local body schools.
- 1,00,000 fresh scholarships are awarded every year across India.

## 3. Eligibility Conditions
- Students whose parental income from all sources is not more than Rs 3,50,000/- per annum are eligible to avail the scholarships.
- The student must have scored minimum 55% marks or equivalent grade in Class VII examination (5% relaxation for SC/ST students).
- Selection is based on a State-Level Mental Ability Test (MAT) and Scholastic Aptitude Test (SAT).

## 4. Disbursement Procedure
- Scholarships are disbursed directly into the bank accounts of students through Public Financial Management System (PFMS) under Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) mode.
- Applications and renewal submissions are handled exclusively through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP: https://scholarships.gov.in).
