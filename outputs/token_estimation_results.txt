Key Observations:
1. Standard English words average ~4 characters and ~1 token or less, with low token/char ratios.
2. Code snippets and punctuation-heavy lines use more tokens per character due to syntax delimiters.
3. Non-ASCII languages (like Hindi) require multiple tokens per single character because tiktoken 
   encodes non-ASCII characters as multi-byte sequences, dramatically increasing costs.
4. Extremely long or compound words are split into subword fragments, yielding higher token counts.
