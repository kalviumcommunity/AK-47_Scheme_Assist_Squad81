--------------------------------------------------
1. Token Size (250 tokens):

   - 250 tokens (~1000 chars) represents a coherent semantic unit (1-2 paragraphs).

2. Controlled Overlap (50 tokens / 20%):
   - Guarantees that key eligibility criteria and full sentences sitting on boundaries
