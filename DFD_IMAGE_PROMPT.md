# SchemeAssist Data-Flow Diagram Image Prompt

Create a professional, editorial civic-tech data-flow diagram for the SchemeAssist welfare eligibility platform. Show how an Indian citizen moves from discovery to eligibility guidance, document submission, application tracking, and helpdesk support.

## Diagram content

- External actors: Citizen, Government Scheme Officer, and Administrator.
- Processes: Public Scheme Discovery, Citizen Profile and Onboarding, AI Eligibility Analysis, Document Upload and Verification, Application Submission and Tracking, AI Assistant and Helpdesk, and Admin Monitoring.
- Data stores: Verified Scheme Knowledge Base, Citizen Profile Store, Document Store, Applications Store, Activity and Helpdesk Logs, and Audit Log.
- Directional arrows must be clear, consistently oriented, and labelled with meaningful data such as profile details, scheme search, eligibility request, source documents, verification result, application status, guidance response, and audit events.

## Composition and style

Use a left-to-right flow on a warm paper-white background. Place external actors at the far left and right, processes as clearly separated navy and teal modules in the middle, and data stores as document or cylinder shapes below the processes. Use a confident deep navy foundation, warm saffron/ochre accents for user actions, restrained green for verified outcomes, and light blue-teal for AI and data services. Use generous whitespace, thin connector lines, subtle shadows, crisp sans-serif typography, and a compact legend. The visual language should feel official, trustworthy, accessible, and suitable for a government product architecture document.

## Exclusions

Do not use fake government or company logos, brand marks that imply official endorsement, decorative 3D effects, clip art, gradients that reduce contrast, crowded arrows, or illegible microtext. Keep all labels short enough to read at presentation size. Do not invent external systems or data flows that are not described above.
